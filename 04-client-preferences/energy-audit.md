# 🔋 Energy Audit

> List everything the executive does in a typical week, then sort it. Anything in **Drains** or **Delegate** moves to the EA first.

| Task | Energizes / Drains | Skill Required (High / Low) | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
|  |  |  | Keep / Delegate to EA / Automate / Eliminate |

### Decision key
- **Keep:** energizes the executive and needs their skill
- **Delegate to EA:** drains them or needs low skill
- **Automate:** repeatable and rule-based
- **Eliminate:** no longer worth doing
