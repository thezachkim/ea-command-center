# 🎯 EA Role Scorecard

## Mission of the Role
> Free up the executive's time and attention so they can focus on their highest-value work.

## Outcomes
| Outcome | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Inbox under control | Unread / unprocessed at EOD | 0 |
| Calendar optimized | Scheduling conflicts | 0 |
| Hours returned to exec | Hours / week saved | 10+ |
| Responsiveness | Avg response to exec | < 1 hr |
| Travel executed | Issues per trip | 0 |

## Competencies
- **Proactive:** anticipates needs
- **Discreet:** handles confidential info
- **Organized:** nothing falls through the cracks
- **Clear communicator**
- **Resourceful problem solver**
