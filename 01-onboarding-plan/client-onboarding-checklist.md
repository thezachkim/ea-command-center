# 🗣️ Client Onboarding Checklist

> The questions to walk through with the executive in the kickoff call (60–90 min). Capture answers in the linked pages.

## Before the Kickoff
- [ ] Send calendar invite + agenda
- [ ] Share this workspace with the executive
- [ ] Prep list of tools needing access

## During the Kickoff
- [ ] What are your top 3 priorities this quarter?
- [ ] What drains your energy / what should I take off your plate first?
- [ ] How do you prefer to communicate (Slack, text, voice note, email)?
- [ ] What does 'urgent' mean to you? Response-time expectations?
- [ ] Who gets immediate access to you? Who should I gatekeep?
- [ ] Working hours, focus blocks, no-meeting times
- [ ] Decision authority: what can I approve without asking? (spend limit $___)
- [ ] Confidential topics or people I should know about

## After the Kickoff
- [ ] Send recap with next steps within 24 hours
- [ ] Fill in Personal Info, Business Info and Client Preferences pages
- [ ] Schedule recurring weekly 1:1
